# Joe's Pizza – Policies

## Refunds

If an item is missing, wrong or arrives damaged, tell us within 24 hours with your order code. We remake the item or refund it, whichever you prefer. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Delivery times

Most deliveries arrive in 30 to 45 minutes. On Friday and Saturday evenings it can take up to 60 minutes. Pickup orders are usually ready in 15 to 20 minutes.

## Cancellations

You can cancel for free until we start preparing your order. After that we cannot cancel, because the food is already being made. Party Catering can be cancelled for free up to 48 hours before the event.

## Privacy

We only keep your name, phone number and address to prepare and deliver your order. We never sell or share them. Ask us at any time to delete your data.
