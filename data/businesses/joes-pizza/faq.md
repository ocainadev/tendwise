# Joe's Pizza – Frequently asked questions

## Opening hours

We are closed on Mondays. Tuesday to Thursday we open from 11:00 to 22:00, Friday from 11:00 to 23:30, Saturday from 12:00 to 23:30 and Sunday from 12:00 to 21:00 (New York time). The last order is taken 15 minutes before closing.

## Delivery

### Area

We deliver within 3 miles of the restaurant. Outside that area you can order for pickup.

### Fees

Delivery costs $3.99. Orders of $40.00 or more are delivered for free. Pickup is always free.

## Payment methods

We accept credit and debit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Cash is accepted for pickup and in the restaurant only, not for delivery.

## Allergens and gluten-free

The Gluten-Free Margherita uses a certified gluten-free crust, but it is baked in the same oven as our regular pizzas, so it is not safe for people with celiac disease. Our dough contains wheat; most dishes contain milk. We do not cook with peanuts or tree nuts. Ask us about any other allergen before you order.

## Order status

To check an order, give us your order code (for example JP-1042) and the last 4 digits of the phone number used for the order.
